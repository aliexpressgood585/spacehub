── PART C: ISOLATED LEVERAGE — where the stop stops protecting you ──
  Isolated margin liquidates at roughly 1/leverage adverse (minus the
  maintenance margin). While that distance is WIDER than the stop, the
  stop fires first and leverage only scales dollars. Once it is
  NARROWER, every loser becomes a full margin wipe instead of a 1R loss.
  15m  median stop 0.50% -> stop is inside liquidation up to **201x**; for the widest 10% of stops only to 107x
  4h   median stop 2.17% -> stop is inside liquidation up to **46x**; for the widest 10% of stops only to 28x

  AND THE PART LEVERAGE CANNOT TOUCH: an R-multiple is leverage-invariant.
  15m nets -0.4082R per trade, 4h nets -0.0508R. At 10x those are
  -4.082R and -0.508R of the account per trade — the same
  numbers, ten times louder. v89bt measured this end to end: 2x -10.8%,
  3x -45.0%, 10x -118.7%, 100x -342.1%. Leverage is a volume knob.
