── WHAT 1000%/WEEK WOULD REQUIRE, against these rows ──
  1000%/week = 11x per week = roughly 5.7e54x over three years.
  The best row above is the number to compare it with.

  READ "RUIN" FIRST. Six independent $10,000 windows; RUIN counts the
  ones that ended as a dead account. A row with a high net% and a
  non-zero RUIN is not an opportunity — it is a coin flip that paid
  out in the windows that survived.
  STILL OPTIMISTIC, and by a lot: liquidation is checked once per
  management bar, not tick by tick; there is no funding spike, no
  auto-deleveraging, no exchange outage and no widening spread in a
  crash — every one of which makes real leveraged trading worse.
