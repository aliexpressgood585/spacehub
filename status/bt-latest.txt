── PARITY DIAGNOSTIC: same signal set as the unconstrained scan ──
  Run 3 showed the LIVE ladder does not reproduce the documented +0.0469R
  even on the same signals. Reading the two implementations side by side
  found why, and it is not a simulator bug — it is a REAL DIVERGENCE:

    LIVE BOT   leg 2 sets trail_sl = entry, then stage 2 ratchets with
               nt = max(cur, chand). The final third can NEVER stop below
               breakeven.
    BACKTESTS  stop = ext - 2.5*atr with ext SEEDED AT ENTRY and updated
               only at the end of each bar, with no floor. On the first bar
               of stage 2 that stop sits near entry - 1.79R.

  Those are different strategies. The looser one gives the final third room
  to dip and recover, which on a fat-tailed edge is worth a great deal. The
  table below measures how much.

  variant                          n     WR%     avgR     vs +0.0469
  breakeven(LIVE) manage 1h      14417   66.3  +0.0713  no
  breakeven(LIVE) manage 4h      14414   65.7  +0.0734  no
  free(BACKTEST) manage 1h       14226   53.5  +0.0423  REPRODUCES
  free(BACKTEST) manage 4h       14235   52.3  +0.0098  no

  WHY MANAGEMENT RESOLUTION IS ON TRIAL HERE, and it is the sharper
  question of the two: every historical backtest manages on 4h bars. A
  trade that banks 0.6R in hour 1 and dips back through entry in hour 3
  of the SAME 4h bar is never breakeven-stopped by that model — the stop
  only becomes active on the NEXT bar. The live bot polls every minute
  and WOULD stop it. So if 4h reproduces +0.0469 and 1h does not, the
  documented edge is partly an artifact of coarse bar resolution, and the
  1h figure is the more faithful estimate of what the bot actually earns.
  That would be a bigger and more uncomfortable finding than the trail
  floor, and it is why it is being measured rather than argued.

  If 'free' reproduces and 'breakeven' does not, then every validated number
  in CLAUDE.md describes a ladder the live bot does not run, and v58bt/v59bt
  (which chose the trailing third and its distance) must be re-run before
  any of them is trusted again. That is a larger finding than the sub-gate
  tier and it is the direct reason shared/strategy.ts was built.
